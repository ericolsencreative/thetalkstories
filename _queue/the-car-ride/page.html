The Car Ride

MY TALK

My father picked me up from basketball practice when I was 13 years old. The car ride home was strangely quiet and uncomfortable. I became nervous he was about to tell me that my parents were getting a divorce. Then, 2 minutes from home, he turns to me and slowly says, "...do you know what a scrotum is?" Stunned, I quietly said, "...I think so...". I didn't. He then responded biologically with, "It's your ball sack." And we finished the ride home in complete and utter silence. I can only imagine he was guilted into having this conversation by my mother whom he later told, 'Yup, I did it! We had the talk. Went great.'
